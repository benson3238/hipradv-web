About HiPrime Advanced

HiPrime Advanced Co., Ltd. (formerly Shigou Technology) is a professional precision parts machining and inspection service provider. We have years of rich experience in CNC turn-milling, automatic lathe machining, and CNC milling.

With top-tier machining equipment and precision measurement instruments, our highly skilled technical team and QA personnel adhere strictly to standardized SOPs to ensure the highest quality products for our clients.

"Implementing the strictest quality control to deliver the best products to our customers."

Advanced Equipment & Inspection

CNC Turn-Milling

Featuring multiple high-precision CNC turn-milling machines and automatic lathes. Flexible machining capacities to complete turning, milling, drilling, and tapping in a single setup.

CNC Multi-axis Milling

Equipped with high-speed and multi-axis milling centers suitable for complex geometric parts development, prototyping, and mid-volume production with fine surface finishes.

Product Applications
